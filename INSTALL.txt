INSPECTION REPORT BUILDER + LEAVE ORDERS
========================================
For Karur Sub Division, Department of Posts

REQUIREMENTS (every office PC already has these)
  - Windows 10 or 11
  - Microsoft Word (any modern version)

INSTALLATION
  1. Copy this entire folder to anywhere on your PC.
     Suggested location:  C:\IR-Builder\
  2. Double-click  "Launch IR Builder.bat"
  3. Your browser opens automatically at the Dashboard.
     Use it like any web page.

WHILE YOU WORK
  - Drafts auto-save every second; you can also click
    "Save now" in the workspace toolbar.
  - The small black "IR Server" window in the taskbar must
    stay open while you use the app (it can stay minimised).

WHEN YOU FINISH
  - Close the browser tab.
  - Click on the launcher window and press ANY KEY to stop
    the server cleanly.

OFFLINE
  - The whole thing runs on your machine. No internet needed.
  - Your data stays in this folder under  app\data\

UPDATING THE OFFICE / STAFF / WORKING-HOURS / MAIL DATA
  Replace the source files in the project root, then in a
  PowerShell window inside the folder, run:
      .\app\Import-Masters.ps1
  This refreshes  app\data\offices.json,  staff.json,  villages.json
