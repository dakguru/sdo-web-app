SB-46 Notice Management
📜 SB-46 notices (Tamil, Word)
One notice per register entry, in register order, each carrying its Speed Post article number beside the serial numbers — the same depositors, accounts and serials the register and the address labels show. The letter details below apply to every notice.
🏷️ Address labels, in duplicate
Two labels per notice on the precut 3 × 6 sheet — the first for the SB-46 cover, the second for the acknowledgement card. Both carry the notice's article number as a scannable barcode and the serial numbers the notice covers in the account list.
📄 Final Report of Cent Percent Verification
Everything marked derived is read straight from the verification lists and this SB-46 register and cannot be typed over. The rest is yours to fill in — the letter particulars, the SBGEN / SBBAS split, the discrepancies you arrived at by reading the replies, and the closing certificate.
