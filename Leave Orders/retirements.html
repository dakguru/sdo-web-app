Superannuation of Departmental staff (60) & discharge of GDS (65) · year-wise

⌛ Loading staff & computing retirement dates…
Retirement dates are computed from date of birth per DoP rules. Departmental: last day of the birth month on attaining 60 (previous month-end if born on the 1st). GDS: the day before the 65th birth-date anniversary.
