Export contacts to your Gmail account

×

Download the three Google CSV files, then import them into Google Contacts. Once imported they sync automatically to the Contacts app on every Android phone, and to iPhone when the Google account is added under Settings → Contacts → Accounts.

  1. Download the Departmental, GDS and Outsiders CSV files above — one at a time.
  2. Open Google Contacts in a browser and sign in with your Gmail account.
  3. In the left sidebar choose Import → Select file → pick a downloaded .csv → Import.
  4. Repeat for the other two files. Each set lands in its own label — Karur SDO - Departmental, Karur SDO - GDS, Karur SDO - Outsiders — so you can review or remove a whole set in one go.
  5. On your phone, make sure contact sync is switched on for that Google account. The numbers appear within a minute.
