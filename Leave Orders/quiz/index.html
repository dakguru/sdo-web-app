Employee Sign-in

This quiz is open to all officials of Karur Division. Sign in with your Google account, then confirm your Employee ID once — your name, designation and office are filled in automatically from the divisional employee list.

How the quiz works
📝
Examination format
What to expect on the day
  • 1Multiple-choice questions with exactly four options; one is correct.
  • 2Each question carries a time limit of its own, and the paper has an overall duration. Whichever runs out first applies.
  • 3There is negative marking for a wrong answer. An unanswered question scores zero — so a pure guess can cost you.
  • 4Questions and options are randomised, so no two employees see the same paper in the same order.
  • 5One question appears at a time, and you cannot return to a question once it is answered or its time expires.
  • 6Answers are saved on the server the moment you choose them. A refresh, a dropped signal or an incoming call puts you back in the same attempt.
✅
Before you begin
A short checklist
  • 📱Use a phone or computer with a stable internet connection. The quiz works on Android Chrome, iPhone Safari and any modern browser.
  • 🔑Keep your Employee ID ready — the 8-digit number on your pay slip or service record.
  • ⌚All examination times shown are Indian Standard Time. The timer is kept by the server, not by your device.
  • 🚫Do not open a second window or device for the same attempt — concurrent sessions are logged for review.
  • 💬If anything goes wrong mid-examination, reopen the same link. Do not start again from scratch.
