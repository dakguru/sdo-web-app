Privacy Notice Last updated 11 October 2026

This notice explains what personal information the National Postal Week 2026 Online Quiz Competition collects from participating officials, why it is collected, who can see it, and how long it is kept. It applies to the quiz portal at krrsdo.vercel.app/Quiz, operated by the Office of the Assistant Superintendent of Post Offices, Karur Division, Karur – 639001, Department of Posts.

1What is collected

● Collected

  • Your verified email address and Google account identifier
  • Your name and profile picture as published by your Google account
  • Your Employee ID, name, designation and office, read from the official Karur Division employee list
  • Your answers, timings, score and rank
  • Technical details of the attempt — server-side submission timestamps, IP address and browser identification

● Never collected

  • Your Google password — it is never seen by this application
  • Your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google service
  • Webcam, microphone or screen monitoring, at any point
  • Location data
  • Any financial, banking or identity document information

Your name, designation and office are not typed in by you. Once you confirm your Employee ID, they are read from the approved divisional employee list, so the record of who sat the examination matches the official records.

2Why it is collected

  • To confirm eligibility — the quiz is open only to officials of Karur Division, verified against the divisional employee list.
  • To make sure one employee holds one registration, and that an Employee ID cannot be claimed by someone else.
  • To conduct the examination — serving questions, keeping the timers and recording answers.
  • To evaluate it fairly — calculating scores, ranks and reports.
  • To audit it — so that a disputed result can be re-checked against what was actually recorded at the time.

No information is used for marketing, profiling or any purpose unconnected with the conduct and audit of this examination, and none is sold or shared with any third party.

3Signing in with Google

Sign-in uses Google’s own authentication. You enter your Google password on Google’s page, never on ours, and this application never receives it.

We request only the three basic permissions Google grants by default — openid, email and profile. No access to Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other service is requested at any time.

Holding a Google account proves nothing about employment. It establishes only that you control that account; your eligibility is decided separately, against the divisional employee list.

4Who can see your information

  • You — your own registration, your own attempt and your own result. The portal is built so that one participant cannot read another’s record, and this is enforced on the server, not merely hidden in the page.
  • Authorised quiz administrators of Karur Division — registrations, participation and results, for conducting the examination and preparing reports. Administrative access is granted individually, not to every portal user.
  • Nobody else. Results are not published to a public leaderboard or shared outside the Division unless the competent authority directs otherwise.

5How long it is kept

  • Examination records — registrations, attempts, answers and results are kept for the conduct and audit of this examination and for any consequent award or review.
  • Sign-in sessions expire automatically and are deleted.
  • Activity logs are kept for review of the examination and then cleared.

6How it is protected

  • All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS.
  • Your Google identity token is verified on the server against Google’s published signing keys before any session is created.
  • Examination data is held in a database that cannot be reached from any browser. It is accessible only to the portal’s own server-side functions.
  • Administrator actions and changes are recorded in an audit log.
  • No more personal information is collected than the examination requires.

7Corrections and withdrawal

If your name, designation or office is shown incorrectly, or an Employee ID has been registered in error, contact the administrator using the details below. Corrections to the employee directory are made by administrators and recorded in the audit log.

You may choose not to participate. If you wish your registration to be withdrawn before the examination, ask the administrator to cancel it.
